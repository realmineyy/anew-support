IF YOU NEED SOMEONE NOW

Real support, free of charge

  • Immediate emotional crisis (US): call or text 988. If anyone is in immediate danger, contact local emergency services.
  • Alcohol or substance support (US): SAMHSA's free, confidential treatment referral line, 1-800-662-HELP (4357).
  • Smoking or vaping (US): 1-800-QUIT-NOW for confidential coaching.
  • Gambling (US): National Problem Gambling Helpline, 1-800-MY-RESET (697-3738).
  • Outside the United States: find verified local helplines at findahelpline.com.

Phone and text availability varies by location. Anew does not operate, monitor, or receive information from these services. The same list is available inside the app at no cost, without a subscription.

QUICK ANSWERS

Common questions

Where is my data?

Journeys, check-ins, urge logs, journal entries, and setback notes are stored in Anew's private local database on your iPhone. There is no Anew account and no proprietary cloud backup, so nothing is uploaded to us. Deleting the app removes this local data.

Can anyone see what I am working on?

Anew does not send recovery records to us. Anyone with access to your unlocked device, visible widgets, notification previews or shared exports may see information. Widgets can display the journey name and category; use them only where you are comfortable with that visibility. Apple backups depend on your settings.

What are anchors?

Photos, voice recordings or short notes you save to revisit during a difficult moment. They stay in Anew's private storage and are not sent to us; Apple backups depend on your settings. System photo and contact pickers return only what you select. The microphone requires permission to record.

What if I have a setback?

Open the journey and record a setback. You choose whether to reset the clock; the record is kept either way, because the complete history is more useful than an unbroken one. Anew uses neutral language and does not score, grade, or shame you.

How do I restore my subscription?

Choose Restore purchases on the subscription screen, or open Settings → Subscription once you are in the app. Use the same Apple Account that made the original purchase.

How do I cancel?

Open the iPhone Settings app, tap your name, choose Subscriptions, select Anew, and follow Apple's controls. Deleting Anew does not cancel a subscription.

What does Anew cost?

Anew is subscription-only: US $3.99 per week, $6.99 per month, or $49.99 per year, with a 3-day free trial for eligible new subscribers. Prices vary by country; Apple shows the localized price before purchase. One subscription unlocks the full app. Support resources, export and erasure remain available without paying.

Is the crisis information behind the paywall?

No. The crisis and treatment resources are reachable straight from the subscription screen, without paying and without starting a trial. We will not put a helpline behind a payment.

Does Anew treat addiction?

No. Anew helps you notice patterns, record what happened, and get through a difficult moment. It is not a medical device and is not a substitute for a doctor, therapist, counsellor, or treatment programme.

How do I delete everything?

Because there is no account or server, there is no profile for us to delete. Open Settings → Your data → Erase everything to delete every journey, check-in, urge, journal entry and setback from the device. You can also delete a single journal entry, or a whole journey, by pressing and holding it. Deleting the app removes its local container too.

Can I keep a copy of my records?

Settings → Your data → Export records creates a JSON file of recovery records and anchor captions/metadata. It does not include photos, audio, preferences or contacts and is not a complete backup. Export and erasure are also reachable from the subscription screen without paying. Manage any Apple backups and previously shared files separately.
