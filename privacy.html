PRIVACY

Recovery records are intimate. They stay yours.

Anew stores recovery records on your device, without an Anew account or advertising. Subscription services process purchase information, not recovery entries. Apple backups and copies you choose to share depend on your settings.

Effective September 14, 2026 · Applies to Anew and this website

Overview

Anew is operated by Aiden Smith (“we,” “us,” or “our”), an independent developer. This policy covers the Anew iOS app and this support website. Contact: aidenjsmith2@icloud.com. Journeys, daily check-ins, urge logs, journal entries, setback records, milestones, and preferences are stored locally in the app's private container on the user's device.

Habit and recovery records may reveal health or other sensitive information. The app processes these records locally, not on a recovery-content server operated by us. This does not mean that all information associated with Anew is anonymous: purchases, website requests and support correspondence are described separately below. Aiden Smith is responsible for the personal information processed for the operation of Anew and this website, subject to the independent responsibilities of Apple and other providers.

Data we collect

Anew does not automatically transmit your journeys, check-ins, urges, triggers, journal entries, setbacks, motivations, chosen contact, photos or voice anchors to us or RevenueCat. Information you enter is used on your device to provide the features you choose. If you send us an email or deliberately share a file, the recipient receives what you include; avoid sending sensitive recovery content to support.

Anew contains no advertising or cross-app tracking. It does not create an Anew account or send recovery records to our servers. RevenueCat provides subscription verification and subscription analytics, as described below. Opening an external resource or sharing an export involves the service you choose.

Local data

Recovery entries live in Anew's private on-device database. Preferences such as the selected theme and reminder settings are stored in the app's own preference storage. Deleting the app generally deletes this local app data. Settings also provides a reset that clears journeys and entries while keeping the app installed.

Anew does not upload, sync, or back up recovery records to us. If the user has enabled an Apple device backup, that backup is governed by Apple's terms and the user's own iCloud or local-backup settings rather than by Anew.

Settings → Your data offers a JSON export of recovery records and anchor captions/metadata. It excludes photo and audio files, preferences and contacts, and is not a complete backup or an import feature. Export and erasure are also available from the subscription screen without an active subscription. Previously exported copies and Apple backups must be managed separately.

Widgets and the shared app group

To draw a Home Screen or Lock Screen widget, Anew writes a small progress snapshot to a shared app group container on the same device. That snapshot contains the selected journey's display name and category label, its start date, the next milestone's name and date, calculated savings, and the selected theme. It remains on the device and is not transmitted to us.

A widget may be visible to anyone who can see the Home Screen or Lock Screen. Renaming a journey does not hide its category on all widget sizes. For discretion, remove the widget or use iOS visibility controls. The shared container is for Anew and its widget, not Taelo or other apps. It also stores the selected theme and a subscription-access deadline used to avoid displaying paid progress after access expires.

Anchors: photos and voice recordings

Anew lets a user save photos, voice recordings and short written notes as “anchors”, shown back to them during a difficult moment. These are the most personal things the app holds, and they are treated accordingly.

Photos and voice recordings are stored in Anew's private container and are not sent to us or RevenueCat. They may be included in Apple device backups, depending on your settings. Anew uses the system photo picker, which returns the image you select without granting access to your whole library. The microphone is used while you record a voice anchor, after iOS permission.

Deleting an anchor removes both the record and its local file. So does erasing everything. The JSON export contains anchor captions and metadata but not image or audio data. Exporting records does not preserve those files; manage any original photos and Apple device backups separately.

Reaching someone you trust

A user can nominate one person to contact in a difficult moment. Anew does not request access to the address book: the system contact picker runs outside the app and returns only the name and number of the single contact chosen. That name and number are stored on the device and are never transmitted to us.

Choosing to reach out opens Messages, or the phone dialler, with the text already written. Anew never sends a message or places a call on a user's behalf — the user sends it, or does not. Once handed to Messages or the dialler, the conversation is governed by Apple and the user's carrier rather than by Anew.

Notifications

Daily and pattern reminders are optional and scheduled locally by iOS. No recovery reminder is sent through our servers or a push service we operate. Daily reminder wording can be previewed in Settings and does not name the behaviour being changed. Pattern reminders use on-device urge records to choose a reminder time. A trial-ending reminder may be scheduled using iOS provisional notification permission; you can turn notifications off in iOS Settings. App names and notification previews can still reveal that you use Anew. Delivery is not guaranteed and reminders are not emergency monitoring.

Subscriptions and RevenueCat

Anew requires a subscription, which Apple processes through the App Store. Anew uses StoreKit to determine whether access is currently active on the Apple Account signed in on the device. Apple may provide developers with transaction, proceeds, territory, and subscription-status reports under Apple's own terms and privacy policy. We do not receive payment-card details.

Anew also uses RevenueCat to verify subscription entitlements and provide subscription reporting. RevenueCat receives a pseudonymous subscription identifier, product and transaction metadata, entitlement status, and limited app and device metadata needed to operate that service. Anew does not send journeys, check-ins, urge logs, triggers, journal entries, setbacks, motivations or anchors to RevenueCat.

Anew does not assign a customer identifier based on your name, email address or recovery records, and does not send identifying customer attributes to RevenueCat. The automatically generated subscription support ID is pseudonymous, not a promise of irreversible anonymity. If you choose to email that ID to support, we can associate your message with your subscription record for that request. An Anew purchase may itself suggest an interest in habit change; it does not tell us which habit you selected.

Support correspondence

If you email support, we receive your email address, message, attachments and any subscription support ID you provide. Use a description of the technical problem rather than health or recovery details. We use correspondence to respond, investigate the issue and keep necessary support, security or legal records, not for advertising. Our mailbox is hosted by Apple iCloud Mail. If you send unnecessary sensitive content, you can ask us to remove it; we may also ask for a redacted replacement and delete the unnecessary content, subject to applicable retention obligations.

External resources

The app and this site link to independent crisis and treatment services, including telephone helplines and findahelpline.com. Choosing such a link opens the phone or browser and hands the interaction to that organisation, whose own privacy practices then apply. We do not operate, monitor, or receive any information from those services, and we are not told which resources a user contacts.

Website hosting

This support website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may process your IP address, browser information and request logs to deliver and secure the website, under its privacy statement. We do not add advertising, analytics scripts, non-essential cookies, external font requests or a contact form. Visiting the website is a network request, unlike opening the bundled legal documents in the app. Emailing us is optional.
